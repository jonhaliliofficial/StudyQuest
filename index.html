A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SPACE

Meet StudyQuest

A study planner with a little game spirit, built to make the next step easier to see.

PLAN · FOCUS · GROW

Make studying feel
less overwhelming.

StudyQuest turns a big pile of schoolwork into smaller quests. You plan what matters, focus on one task at a time, and see your effort build up through XP, ranks and progress history.

+25 XPONE STEP AT A TIME

What you can do here

The main tools, without the clutter.

Turn work into quests

Create tasks, group them by subject, set priorities and due dates, then filter the list to see what needs attention.

Make room for focus

Use a simple focus timer, pick the quest you're working on and record sessions as part of your study history.

Earn your next rank

Complete quests and focus sessions to earn XP. Your rank path gives the effort a visible milestone to work toward.

See your progress

Track completed work, focus minutes, streaks and small achievements so you can see patterns over time.

Make it feel like yours

Choose a color theme, font, text size, clock format and study buddy cat. Your choices stay saved on this device.

Private by design

This version runs in your browser. It has no online account or cloud sync, and your profile and tasks are stored locally.

How the study loop works

Four simple steps, then repeat whenever you need.

Small progress still counts.

StudyQuest isn't here to make every day perfect. It's here to help you work out the next step and give yourself credit for taking it.
